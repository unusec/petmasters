# Aberrant familiars usage
Aberrant familiars are used for:
- Legendary skills
- Stacking advanced (orange) traits
- Conversion stones

## Legendary skills
The most popular usage. A lot of people know that Savior skill is useful in PvP. But Savior can only be legendary, it doesn't have magic or rare versions. And this is the main reason why aberrant Duskprowler is better than any ordinary Duskprowler - aberrant Duskprowler can have Savior, ordinary Duskprowler can have only limited rare skills.

Aberrant familiar is still better even when both ordinary and aberrant familiar can have the same skill, for example rare Lingering Wounds. Aberrant Broodtalon gives a path to turn rare Lingering Wounds to legendary Lingering Wounds with a noticeably higher multiplier 38.8%, while ordinary Broodtalon can have at best enhanced rare Lingering Wounds with a lower multiplier 19.2%

## Stacking advanced traits
Ordinary familiars are cheaper if you want to stack 1-2 advanced traits. But aberrant familiars become the only option for high paying players who want to stack 3 or more advanced traits.
The reason is that an aberrant with 4 legendary skills and 3 advanced traits is relatively easier to find compared to an ordinary familiar with 4 enhanced rare skills and 3 advanced traits. Aberrant familiar with 4 legendary skills are often sold on market for cheap. While new ordinary familiars can't be sold on the market, and building your own ordinary familiar with 3 advanced traits requires to win a very small RNG chance.

## Conversion stones
Sometimes they provide the cheapest way to get conversion stones. If you buy a familiar with 2 legendary and 0 rare skills for less than 20 000 platinum, you can downvert it - convert without conversion stones. The downvert outcome often becomes 1L5R that gives 32 stones while you spend for example 21 000 platinum (15 000 platinum on 2L0R familiar, 4 000 platinum on 2 cheapest familiars and 2 000 platinum to convert).
In the past it was popular to downvert 1L0R to 6R and 32 stones, but this was disabled by one of the patches. Today the only cheap way is to dowvert 2L0R.
