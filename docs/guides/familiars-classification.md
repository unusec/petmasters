# Familiar Classifications & Quality Tiers

In Diablo Immortal, familiars are classified by their **Type** (Normal/Ordinary vs. Aberrant) and their **Quality Tier** (Common, Magic, Rare, Legendary).

***

## 1. Familiar Type Classification

### 🔵 Normal / Ordinary Familiars

* **Market Status**: Cannot be listed or sold on the market.
* **Skill Caps**: Can have Blue (normal) skills or Enhanced Rare skills.
* **Trait Caps**: Can unlock Legendary Traits if they possess an Enhanced Rare skill.
* **Obtainability**:
  * Contracts bought from **Nisza** NPC for 10,000 gold.
  * Random contract drops from farming Open World, Dungeons, Lairs, and Towers.
  * Directly bought from Nisza's shop stock in Westmarch.

### 🔴 Aberrant Familiars

* **Market Status**: **Can be bought and sold on the market.**
* **Skill Caps**: Can possess **Legendary Skills** (Red with purple border).
* **Trait Caps**: Can possess Legendary Traits.
* **Obtainability**:
  * Nisza's shop stock in Westmarch.
  * Marketplace purchases.
  * **Bonus 20% Chance**: When making a normal conversion with ordinary familiars, you have a 20% chance to receive a extra Aberrant familiar with a random race.

***

## 2. Familiar Quality Classification

The visual form of your familiar in-game changes based on its **primary attributes**, **skills**, and **traits**.

Attributes are the most critical factor—they determine the power scaling of skills, activate traits, and control the familiar's physical appearance.

```
       +-------------------------------------------------+
       |                  Attributes                     |
       +-------------------------------------------------+
           /                   |                     \
          v                    v                      v
[ 1st Form: Common/Magic ]  [ 2nd Form: Rare ]  [ 3rd Form: Legendary ]
   (Attribute < 120)       (Attribute >= 120)     (Attribute >= 170)
```

| Quality Tier  | In-Game Icon | Attributes Required        | Visual Form & Conditions                                             |
| ------------- | ------------ | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Common**    | Grey         | Primary Attribute < 70     | 1st Base Form                                                        |
| **Magic**     | Blue         | Primary Attribute 70 – 119 | 1st Base Form                                                        |
| **Rare**      | Yellow       | Primary Attribute ≥ 120    | 2nd Advanced Form (Requires Rare Skill)                              |
| **Legendary** | Red          | Primary Attribute ≥ 170    | 3rd Final Form (Requires Legendary/Enhanced Skill & Legendary Trait) |
