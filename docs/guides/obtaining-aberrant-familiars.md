# Aberrant familiars
Aberrant familiars are those with the purple icon in the top left corner.
You can also filter them using the Aberrant Familiars menu item in the dropdown.

Advantages:
- can have legendary skills like Savior;
- can have other legendary skills that are noticeably stronger than their rare versions;
- can have rare, advanced, utility traits, advanced traits can be stacked;
- can be sold and bought;

Disadvantages:
- conversions cost 2000 platinum and 2,8,30,40,50,60 stones depending on the number of legendary skills;
- if you forget to click the conversion stones checkbox, your familiar might lose its skills and you will have no option to revert;

# How to get aberrant familiars
- Buying from Nisza for 2000, 4000, 8000, 16000 platinum. Usually aberrant familiars for 2000 platinum are the most popular. Others can be bought too if they have legendary 170+ attributes or have useful rare skills.
- Buying on the market if someone sells them. The price is decided by supply and demand. You need to know what skills are good to answer if the purchase is worth the price.
- 20% chance when you convert 3 ordinary familiars.
- In-game shop sometimes features bundles with Aberrant Darkling Vessel: Spiritcaller's Bundle, Spiritbinder's Bundle.
