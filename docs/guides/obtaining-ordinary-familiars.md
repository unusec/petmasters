# Ordinary familiars
Ordinary familiars are those that don't have the purple icon in the top left corner.
You can also filter them using the Ordinary Familiars menu item in the dropdown.

Advantages:
- can have rare, advanced, utility traits, and advanced traits can be stacked;
- can generate new utility traits with 10% chance;
- conversions cost 0 platinum and 0 stones;
- conversions keep the attributes of the best familiar in the conversion;
- conversions keep or upgrade the rarity of skills of the best familiar;

Disadvantages:
- can't have legendary skills like Savior;
- have enhanced rare skills that are noticeably weaker than legendary skills;
- new ordinary familiars aren't tradable, only a few legacy familiars from 2024 can be traded;

# How to get ordinary familiars
- The most popular way is summoning from contracts and then leveling up to level 30 using Spirit Essence. This is free and the most recommended way to get ordinary familiars.
- Buying from Nisza for 2000, 4000, 8000, 16000 platinum. Sometimes it can be useful to buy them for 2000 platinum to get conversion stones. More expensive ordinary familiars are hardly ever useful, the only exception if they have 170+ attributes.  
- Buying on the market if someone sells legacy ordinary familiars from 2024. Can be a good purchase if they cost less than 2000 platinum.

## How to get contracts
- 3 contracts are sold by Nisza every week for 10 000 gold.
- In-game shop sometimes features bundles with contracts: Pact Initiate's Bundle, Pact Adept's Bundle, Pact Master's Bundle. For example, Pact Master's Bundle for $25.99 includes 10 legendary crests, 6 familiar contracts, 400 spirit essence, 20 aspirant's keys.
- In-game events sometimes give contracts for free. For example, the gem upgrade events give 100 sigils per week and you can claim familiar contracts after collecting 100, 300, 500, 800, 1650 sigils.
- The rest can be found by killing elite monsters from 10am to midnight every 30 minutes at xx:00 and xx:30. The highest chance is exactly at xx:30:00 or in the first 30 seconds, but some guides recommend to kill elite monsters for a few minutes.

## How to get spirit essence
- Killing blue and yellow elite monsters. Their highest concentration can be found in accursed towers or in verge. Restart the verge until you see "Additional elites will appear this round";
- Ending pact with a level 30 ordinary familiar returns 11 spirit essence;
- In-game shop bundles with contracts also include spirit essence;
