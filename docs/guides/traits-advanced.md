Also known as legendary traits or orange traits.
The same advanced trait on multiple different bonded familiars stacks and gains increased value.

## 2. Primary Attributes per Familiar Species
Traits are activated either by primary or secondary attributes of the familiar. 
You can click at the inactive trait to see what attribute it requires. For example, Varog traits can require either Agility or Insight.
Orange traits require attribute value 170 which is usually hard to get.
Preferably you need orange traits activated by the primary attribute because Varog with 170+ Agility is relatively cheap, 20-50k platinum, but Varog with 170+ Insight is very expensive, 200-300k platinum.
Primary (good) attributes for each familiar:
- **Ferocity**: Quakestrider, Myrrjen
- **Agility**: Stormlost, Varog
- **Insight**: Broodtalon, Skitterwight, Bramblestalker
- **Resilience**: Duskprowler, Ashsweeper

## 4. The number of traits and chances
The number of orange traits depends on the number of legendary or enhanced rare skills.
For aberrant familiars with legendary skills the distribution of orange traits is this:
- 1 legendary skill - 1 orange trait 100%
- 2 legendary skills - 1 orange trait 50%, 2 orange traits 50%
- 3 legendary skills - 1 orange traits 20%, 2 orange traits 80%
- 4 legendary skills (except 4 legendary 2 rare) - 2 orange traits 50%, 3 orange traits 50%
- 4 legendary 2 rare skills - 2 orange traits 75%, 3 orange traits 25% (rare skills make it worse)
- 5 legendary skills - 2 orange traits 20%, 3 orange traits 80%
- 6 legendary skills - predicted 3 orange traits 50%, 4 orange traits 50%

For ordinary familiars with enhanced rare skills the distribution is this:
- 1 enhanced rare skill - 1 orange trait 100%
- 2 enhanced rare skills - 1 orange trait 55%, 2 orange traits 45%
- 3 enhanced rare skills - 1 orange trait 36%, 2 orange traits 64%
- 4 enhanced rare skills - predicted 1 orange trait 20%, 2 orange traits 60%, 3 orange traits 20%
- 5 enhanced rare skills - predicted 1 orange trait 20%, 2 orange traits 48%, 3 orange traits 32%
- 6 enhanced rare skills - predicted 1 orange trait 20%, 2 orange traits 40%, 3 orange traits 40%, 4 orange traits 0%

For ordinary familiars prioritize those with 3 or more enhanced rare skills to get more orange traits. Ordinary familiars don’t require conversion stones to reroll which makes 3E rerolls as cheap as 2E rerolls, and higher probabilities to get 2 orange traits. 

For aberrant familiars prioritize 2L because each reroll costs 8 conversion stones while 3L rerolls cost 30 conversion stones. If you need 3 orange traits, prioritize 4L0R, 4L1R, 5L, 6L, but avoid 4L2R.
