# Utility traits
Also known as purple traits.
There are currently 3 utility traits:
1. Salvager. The menu item opens a window to select individual items or to salvage all. Your familiar removes all selected items from your inventory, disappears for a few seconds, then returns with scrap and dust.
2. Appraiser. The menu item opens a window to select individual legendary or set items to identify. Your familiar removes all selected items from your inventory, disappears for a few seconds, then returns with identified items. If your inventory doesn’t have space for returned items, they will be mailed to you.
3. Pack Mule. The menu item opens the armory window with your saved loadouts. Your familiar will swap the selected loadout in the same way as you can do near the armory. Note that monster attacks or skills on cooldown will prevent loadout change.

Utility traits on ordinary familiars:
- 25% chance to stay per each utility trait if any existing familiar already has a utility trait
- 10% chance to appear on any ordinary convert

Utility traits on aberrant familiars:
- 25% chance to stay per each utility trait if any existing familiar already has a utility trait
- New utility traits will never appear if none of the source familiars has utility traits

## Stacking multiple utility traits on a single familiar
To stack 2 or 3 different utility traits on the same familiar, you need to find 2-3 familiars with different utility traits and convert them. This will give 42.2% chance to keep 1 utility trait, 14% chance to keep 2 utility traits, 1.6% chance to keep 3 utility traits.
- Based on the sample of 12 converts, the same duplicate utility trait on 3 source familiars (Salvager-Salvager-Salvager, or Appraiser-Appraiser) does not increase the chance for this purple utility trait to stay. For example, 3 familiars with 3 Salvagers will have the same 25% chance to keep Salvager as 3 familiars when one familiar as Salvager and 2 familairs have empty traits. Don't duplicate the same utility traits in your converts
- The priority is yellow > purple > orange traits. If the familiar generates 2 yellow traits - then 3 purple traits become impossible.
- Ideally you want only orange and purple traits, for example 2 orange 2 purple, or 1 orange 3 purple. This is possible only on aberrant familiars with 0 rare skills: 1L0R, 2L0R, 3L0R, 4L0R, 5L0R, 6L.
- Ordinary familiars can generate 1-3 yellow traits even if it is 2E0R, and these yellow traits will override purple traits. This is why orange & purple traits stacking is better to be done on aberrant familiars, not on ordinary familiars.
