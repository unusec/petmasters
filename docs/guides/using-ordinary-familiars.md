# Ordinary familiars usage
Ordinary familiars are used for:
- Converting 170+ attributes from one familiar to another
- Stacking advanced (orange) traits
- Conversion stones
- Free aberrant familiars with 20% chance

## Converting 170+ attributes from one familiar to another
For example, you bought a 200 Insight Bramblestalker and want to turn it into 200 Resilience for your current Duskprowler.
The cheapest way is:
- Attribute exchange from your aberrant to ordinary Bramblestalker;
- Convert 200 Insight ordinary Bramblestalker with ordinary Duskprowlers - no platinum cost, no stones cost, and the result familiar keeps high attributes;
- After 1 or more converts you get an ordinary Duskprowler with 200 Resilience;
- Attribute exchange to your PvP Duskprowler;

The alternative is aberrant conversion. A lot more expensive when the cheapest aberrant Duskprowler costs 4000 platinum and you keep losing 33%:66%. The only advantage is a very small chance that RNG rolls Savior Duskprowler that you can sell.
If the goal is to transfer attributes, normal familiars provide a much cheaper way. If you expect you might need to do this process in the future - always keep 4-6 normal Duskprowlers in your inventory. This is also the case when buying ordinary Duskprowlers from Nisza for 2000 platinum can be a good idea.

## Stacking advanced traits
If you want to stack multiple advanced traits, for example both Maim and Bestial Might, or both Prescience and Finesse, you need multiple familiars with at least 2 advanced traits each, and ideally with both traits on primary attribute. Getting aberrant familiars with the right traits will become expensive very fast, you need at least 2 legendary skills, the chance to get 2 or more useful advanced traits is very low, and each conversion costs 2000 platinum, 8 conversion stones and expensive aberrant familiars. Getting ordinary familiars with the right traits can be done for free in the long run, you only need free ordinary familiars from contracts.

## Getting conversion stones
If you summon ordinary familiars on your alt account that doesn't stack traits, you might prefer to end pact every outcome for stones.

To maximize the number of conversion stones, do the following for each familiar:
- 0R, 1R, 2R - convert with other familiars in the same category, they give few stones but have a high chance to upgrade;
- 3R, 4R, 5R, 6R - end pact, they give multiple stones and almost never upgrade;
- 1E0R - convert with 1E0R and only with 1E0R;
- 1E, 2E, 3E - end pact, assuming you don’t need traits;
- 4E, 5E, 6E - keep just because they are very rare;

The conversion of 3 summoned from contracts familiars gives 6.2 conversion stones on average.

If conversion stones are the goal, it might be a good idea to buy ordinary familiars from Nisza or on the market for 2000 platinum each. You spend 6000 platinum on 3 ordinary familiars, you get 6.2 stones with 20% aberrant chance from each conversion on average. Note: this is profitable only in the long run, you might lose a lot of platinum and gain very few stones at the beginning, but it should become the expected value over hundreds of conversions.

## Getting free aberrant familiars
Every ordinary conversion can spawn an aberrant familiar with 20% chance. The spawned aberrant familiar can have between 0 and 6 rare skills, can be magic, rare and legendary. Most often a conversion spawns a magic aberrant familiar with 0 rare skills.

The choice what to do with spawned aberrant familiars depend on your market prices. Usually it is more profitable to sell every aberrant for the maximum you can sell. Exceptions are:
- The market pays <3000 platinum for the cheapest aberrants, but pays 30000 for any 2L and 60000 for any 3L. In this case always convert 3 aberrant familiars using 2 stones.
- You would buy the same aberrant familiars. For example, if you have a 2L Duskprowler and need cheap aberrant Duskprowlers to get Savior.
