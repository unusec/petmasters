# How to Contribute & Suggest Updates

We welcome all to contribute knowledge/corrections/updates to our guide!

---

## ✏️ Direct In-Site Editor (No GitHub Needed!)

1. Navigate to any page on this site.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **"✏️ Edit / Suggest Change directly on site"**.
3. A modal editor will pop up right on your screen.
4. Enter your **Discord handle** and brief summary of what you added or fixed.
5. Click **"Submit Suggestion for Mod Review"**.

---

## 🛡️ Discord Moderator Approval Process

When a suggestion is submitted:
1. An automated notification alert is posted in our [PetMasters Discord Community](https://discord.gg/FKxn3HVW3d) mod channel.
2. Discord moderators review the proposed changes.
3. Once approved by a mod, the guide updates live on the website automatically!
